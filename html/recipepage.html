Samosa Chat

Samosa is one of India's most beloved snacks, known for its crispy, golden-brown outer crust and deliciously spiced potato filling. Made with a flaky pastry covering stuffed with mashed potatoes, green peas, and aromatic Indian spices, samosas are perfect for evening tea, family gatherings, or simply satisfying your cravings. Serve them hot with mint chutney or tangy tamarind chutney for the perfect combination of flavours!

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups of all-purpose flour( maida)
  • 4 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 1/2 teaspoon carom seeds (ajwain)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • water (as required)

For the potato filling

  • 4 medium-sized potatoes (boiled and mashed)
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 2 green chillies (finely chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 teaspoon dry mango powder (amchur)
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Salt to taste

For frying

  • Oil (as required)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the dough using flour,oil,salt and water.
  2. Boil adn mash potatoes
  3. Cook potatoes with peas and spices
  4. Roll the dough and shape it into the cones.
  5. Fill the cones with potato mixture and seal them.
  6. Deep-fry until golden brown and crispy.
  7. Serve hot with chutney.
